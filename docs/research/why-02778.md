# What the disputed GEMSDOE32 H33 “0.2778” does—and does not—mean

**Review date:** 2026-10-11 UTC. **Evidence class:** `PUBLIC-LEADERBOARD-SNAPSHOT` plus owner-maintained
artifact metadata; no organizer score receipt was found.

## Verified facts

- The official [DrivenData GEMS leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/)
  displayed **extradr19 at rank 23 with 0.2778** in the 2026-10-11 review. This is a participant row.
  It does not contain an artifact filename, SHA-256, or H33 submission identifier.
- The GEMSDOE32 [owner landing page](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/index.html)
  marks H33-2-B2 `UNSCORED` and says the project's artifact has no organizer score. It labels 0.2747
  a projected/model value, not a returned competition result.
- The owner's [H33 audit JSON](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/downloads/gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-audit.json)
  identifies H33-2-B2 by SHA-256
  `c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9`; it records
  `predicted_leaderboard_dti: 0.27467315976932155` and `slot_decision: SLOT_ELIGIBLE`. Those fields
  are owner-side audit/projection metadata, not an organizer score receipt. `SLOT_ELIGIBLE` does not
  mean scored.
- The same public-board observation contained unrelated participant rows at 0.2747 (ranks 30–32),
  so the rounded projection value is not unique evidence of H33 attribution.
- An owner-maintained leaderboard page is an October 4 snapshot; it is stale relative to the
  2026-10-11 live public board. A same-day earlier fetch in this repository recorded DARD at rank 8;
  a later fetch recorded rank 9, illustrating that the board is dynamic.

## Answer

The repository can explain the **dispute**, but it cannot verify a score of 0.2778 for H33. The
0.2778 value belongs to the `extradr19` participant row in the observed public board. No verified
receipt or hash crosswalk ties that row to H33-2-B2. GEMSDOE32's owner page instead calls H33
unscored and 0.2747 a projection. The supportable conclusion is **unsubstantiated attribution**.
A participant-row mix-up is one possible explanation, but the available evidence does not establish
how the attribution arose or why `extradr19` received 0.2778.

This is not proof that a private score could never exist. It is a statement about the evidence found:
without an organizer receipt, submission ID, and artifact-hash crosswalk, the H33 claim is not
`ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`.
