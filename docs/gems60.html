Exact-metric pattern selection

Each candidate geometry was scored with the exact published metric on a synthetic segment (60 px long, truth scattered with the sibling's measured 1.85 px texture and, as a sensitivity case, a sharp 0.5 px trace) over across-strike bias 0–3 px, and the marginal utility U = (1 − 0.2·DTI₀)·ΔTP − 0.2·DTI₀·ΔFP at DTI₀ = 0.26. The bias classes are then weighted by the measured σ distribution above — the σ-weighted expectation is the selector.

Negative result recorded this session (a deliverable, not a footnote)

“Cross-family agreement is the best local ranking statistic” was falsified on this instrument: at an equal 40 000-dot budget the agreement count ranks no better than a single family's response (mean exact kernel credit {'all_families_strength': 0.0107, 'family_magnetic': 0.009, 'family_gravity': 0.0098, 'family_topographic': 0.0202, 'family_subsurface': 0.0082}), and stand-alone AUC against withheld truth is 0.48–0.58 per family (no leakage, but no signal either). The agreement count therefore survives only as the allocator's ranking weight, never as a hard gate — and that is stated here rather than hidden.
