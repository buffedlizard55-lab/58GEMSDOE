What this submission is (method, one paragraph)

Candidate fault ridge axes are extracted from (a) the visible USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue skeleton and (b) the independent SGMC public fault compilation where it runs within the measured 8 px localization cap of the catalogue. Per the lane, emission uses parallel dotted lines about ±sigma wide (spacing near the 3-px kernel radius), each dot at exactly 1.0, off the mapped catalogue.
