0. Method notes (what was actually run)

  • Ridge fields: Hessian of a Gaussian-smoothed band, scale 1 px (100 m features) and 2 px (200 m features). The 1.5 px scale in the original plan was not used; this is a documented deviation (RESOLUTIONS in src/gems/ribbon.py).
  • Ridge bands: total magnetic (tmi_hg), isostatic gravity (iso_grav_anom_hg), and topographic slope (det_elev_slope), each at fine and coarse scale. Values are clipped to the 1st–99th percentile and standardised before the Hessian.
  • σ rule: σ = 1.4826 × MAD of distances from withheld truth to the nearest candidate, counting only matches within 10 px (robust_sigma, run_ribbon_lane.py). The lane's σ rule restricts configurations only when σ < 0.5 (tight line) or σ is 2–3 px (ribbon of ±round(σ)). Every pooled σ selected here was outside those bands, so all configurations were admissible and the utility U alone decided.
  • Emission (E3): all six classes selected 1% of footprint pixels and configuration D3o+1. Pooled σ across blocks: 3.37–4.24 px.
