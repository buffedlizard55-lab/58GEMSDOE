Why this file is NOT cleared, and the one decision that would change it

The protocol's literal stop rule — "more than 70 % of my dots within 3 px of one registry raster's dots" — fires against 81 of the 702 byte-verified witnesses. By the standing instruction that means log as duplicate and stop, and that is what this site does: the banner is red and the file is offered for review only.

It is reported with both readings because the literal rule is provably unsatisfiable for any emitter that places dots on the organizer's own feature structure:

  • 54 firings are information-free. Those witnesses have a chance-level expected overlap E ≥ 0.99 — their 3 px dilation covers essentially the whole footprint, so they overlap every nonempty candidate, including a random dot field. Measured earlier: no buffer from 3 to 10 px clears 0.70 against them (evidence/buffer_overlap_scan.json, IR-58-UNIQ-02/03).
  • 27 firings are against broad dot fields of 155,021–3,380,827 dots (3 %–65 % of the footprint), 18 of them from a single repository that publishes near-duplicate variants of one composite. The worst is 0.8750 against a witness whose own chance expectation is E = 0.8611 — a chance-corrected lift of 0.100, i.e. barely above what random placement would show. The largest lift among all 81 firings is 0.375.
  • Under a lift rule this file clears; under an E-limit rule the threshold decides. Restricting the gate to the 626 witnesses that could have failed to fire (E < 0.70), the worst observed overlap is 0.7777 (13GEMSDOE:docs/downloads/archive/13gems-r6-ensemble-20260929T182914Z.tif, E = 0.6456) — 7 firings. Under a lift rule (observed − E)/(1 − E) > 0.50 there are 2 firings, and every one of them is this repository's own earlier ribbon file — family resemblance, not reuse of another lane (max lift anywhere 0.543).
  • Every discriminating statistic passes by a wide margin: byte-unique True, pixel-unique True, worst rank correlation 0.1824 (limit 0.90) with 0 firings, worst dot-set Jaccard 0.1041 (limit 0.50) with 0 firings. Against the witnesses whose own expected overlap is below 0.30 — the ones that can actually tell two emissions apart — the worst observed overlap is 0.6115 (GEMSDOE50:docs/downloads/gems50-h52-coincidence8-80000-20261007T032938Z.tif).

The decision, which only the owner can make. Either

  1. Ratify a chance-corrected uniqueness rule, of which the lift form is the cleaner one: a witness fires only if (observed − E)/(1 − E) exceeds a stated threshold. At a threshold of 0.50 this file produces 2 firings, and both are this repository's own earlier ribbon files — expected family resemblance, not reuse of another lane — while the highest lift against any other repository is 0.490 (GEMSDOE50:docs/downloads/gems50-h52-coincidence8-80000-20261007T032938Z.tif). An E-limit form ("fire only if E < limit and observed > 0.70") needs a limit of 0.61 or lower to give zero firings, which is a tighter and more arbitrary cut; at E < 0.70 there would still be 7 firings (worst overlap 0.7777 at E = 0.6456).
  2. Keep the literal rule — then no ridge-based or coverage-based emitter in this repository family can ever be submitted, because the registry already contains near-plane-filling witnesses. That is a protocol-level dead end, and it is the honest reading of the instruction as written.

Until the owner ratifies one of these, the file is a reviewable artifact: it is downloadable, format-validated 15/15, and it is not cleared for a slot. Nothing was uploaded and no slot was used.
